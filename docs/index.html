What these numbers mean

Independent days is not the number of days. Two forecasts made a day apart are nearly the same bet, so this counts how much genuinely separate evidence there is — always less, often by half.

Spread check: held at n_eff = 10.0 (needs 20). Errors currently look wider than the claimed sigma, but that comparison is not yet evidence.

The gauge stays near Empty until the real model has been measured over enough independent days to beat a coin flip. A model that has been measured and found wanting sits low too — the label says which.

Prices step in $0.005 for readability, but each probability is computed for its own price rather than rounded off a stored table.
